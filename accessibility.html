Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 11, 2026

S4 Aesthetics LLC is committed to making this website accessible to people with disabilities, and to providing a welcoming experience for every client — online and in person.

Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. This site was built with that target in mind.

What we've done

  • Clear heading structure and semantic markup for screen readers
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Full keyboard navigation, including the menu and product filters
  • Color contrast chosen to meet AA ratios for text
  • Reduced-motion support: animations are disabled for visitors whose devices request it
  • Text that scales with browser and system settings

Third-party content

Some services we link to — such as booking, shopping, or portfolio pages hosted on other platforms — are controlled by third parties, and their accessibility may vary. We take this into account when choosing the platforms we work with.

In person

Because we come to you for fittings, we're glad to accommodate access needs at your location — just let us know what would help when we plan your event.
